01 / THE IDEA

Technology moves fast.
Our values guide us.

WISEowl is a yearlong Rice University campaign supporting the responsible and effective integration of artificial intelligence across campus. It begins with a simple idea: people—not technology—shape our impact.

There is room in the conversation for enthusiastic adopters, cautious explorers, and colleagues designing AI-free approaches. We move forward by asking good questions, learning together, and staying accountable.

05 / A PRACTICAL MINDSET

Pause. Question.
Use your judgment.

Across roles and tools, a few habits help make AI use more thoughtful. Follow official Rice guidance for the details that apply to your work.

01 / CHECK

Know the expectations.

Look at relevant course rules, workplace policies, and approved-tool guidance before you begin.

02 / PROTECT

Handle data carefully.

Protect confidential, sensitive, and personal information and use tools as directed by Rice.

03 / VERIFY

Question the output.

Review facts, context, bias, citations, and suitability before trusting or sharing results.

04 / OWN

Stay accountable.

Keep human review and responsibility at the heart of decisions and final work.
